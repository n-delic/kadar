# AGENTS.md

Guide for AI coding agents (and humans) working in this repo.

## What this is

**Kadar**, a free, open-source (GPL-3.0-or-later) photo and video editor for Android, started for a meme page and released publicly. Everything runs on the phone and offline; the only network use is the in-app image browser (and a one-time download of ML Kit's background-removal model through Play services).

The owner's phone is a Galaxy S25; it also runs on older phones, hence `minSdk 29`.

## What it does today

- **Photo**: open a photo, a blank canvas (`ui/home/BlankDialog.kt` asks its shape, 1080 px on the short side), or web image; add text (meme outline style), emoji, images and saved stickers as layers; move, pinch-resize, rotate (the corner handle and a pinch scale evenly); stretch one way with the handle in the middle of each side, the opposite side kept in place (`Layer.sideDragged`, measured along the layer's own axes: images, stickers and drawings get `Transform.stretch`, the width's scale over the height's, which `drawLayer`, `toLocal`/`shiftedLocal` and so hit tests, handles and content offsets all apply; shapes change `w`/`h`; text drags its wrap; video layers have none; the eraser and crop screens show the picture unstretched); mirror or flip images, stickers and drawings (`Transform.flipX/flipY`: `drawLayer` mirrors the content inside its unflipped box, so handles and hit tests don't change; content-pixel offsets such as a crop or cut-out go through `Transform.shiftedContent`, and the eraser and crop screens show the image flipped); image and sticker opacity (`ImageLayer.opacity`); blend modes for image, text, shape and drawing layers in photos (`ImageLayer.blend` and the same `blend` on `TextLayer`/`ShapeLayer`/`DrawLayer`, a `LayerBlend`: the layer's `drawContent` is wrapped by `withBlend` in one `saveLayer` with that `BlendMode`; `ui/editor/BlendPanel.kt`; not offered in videos, whose layers are drawn apart from the video); an outline around a picture's shape (`ImageLayer.outline` color, `outlineStyle` Solid, Glow or Shadow, `outlineWidth` as a part of the longer side; `ui/editor/ImageOutlinePanel.kt`): `model/ImageOutline.kt` builds an alpha mask from a distance field of the picture's alpha (at most 1024 px on the long side, kept per picture `Bitmap` so a drag's or the export's layer copies don't rebuild it, and per stretch rounded to 10% steps), which `drawContent` draws under the picture in the outline color, outside the vignette and grain, with the opacity on both through one offscreen layer; one effect per image layer, photos and videos alike (`ImageLayer.effect`, an `Effect`: Blur, Pixelate, Posterize, Duotone, Sharpen, Glitch, Pop art, with a strength; `ui/editor/EffectsPanel.kt`, the slider applies on release): `model/Effects.kt` redoes the picture's pixels once and keeps the result per picture `Bitmap` (the last two effects, like the outline masks), which `drawContent` draws over the picture's bounds under the look, sliders, vignette, grain, outline and blend (a blur is worked out small and drawn scaled up); the duotone is linear, so it is a color matrix put before the look on the paint instead; alpha is kept, except where blur, pixelate and glitch move pixels; remove backgrounds on-device; crop the photo the project was opened with (`Project.photoId`; blank canvases have none) and the canvas becomes the cropped picture, at its own resolution, every layer kept at its spot on it, the "Original" aspect too (`Project.fittedTo`, `fittedCanvas`); change canvas aspect (`CANVAS_RATIOS` in `ui/common/Theme.kt`, shared with the video canvas) and background, a color or a blur (`Project.blur`: `model/Backdrop.kt` blurs the bottom image layer small, and the stage, the export and the thumbnail all draw that one bitmap stretched under the layers, through `renderLayers(backdrop =)`); export JPEG/PNG to `Pictures/Kadar` (picked in the export dialog, remembered as `AppSettings.photoFormat`; a transparent canvas is always PNG: `Project.transparent`).
- **Video (NLE)**: a main track of clips in a row (`VideoClip.segments`, each a `Segment` with its own trim, speed (a slider from 0.2x to 10x, caught at 0.5x, 1x and 2x: `speedAt`), volume, keep-pitch and filter/adjust; photos are stills of 3 s, trimmable to 30 s, copied into the project folder). The "+" after the clip strip adds a video or photo, and the Color clip tool a plain-color still (`addColorClip`, canvas-sized); tap a clip to pick it (the clip tools act on it, else on the clip under the playhead), drag the picked clip's ends to trim it (`edgeTrim`, committed on release as one undo step; a second tap opens the full trim strip, whose arrows step the playhead one frame back or forward), long-press-drag to reorder, Split at the playhead, Freeze (`freezeFrame`: a still of the frame under the playhead, from `videoStill`, goes in as a photo clip between the two halves), Save frame (`saveFrame`: that frame, full size, to the gallery as a JPEG), Replace (`replaceClip`: a picked video or photo in its place, keeping its length as far as the new one goes, speed, volume, look and transition), Mute/Unmute (volume 0 or back to full), Crop, Rotate 90°, Mirror and Flip (`Segment.crop` of the source frame, then `flipX`/`flipY` in the source's own axes, then `turns` quarter turns clockwise: `shapeEffects`, a Media3 `Crop` and a `ScaleAndRotateTransformation` before the look, so the fit, the transitions and the vignette use the clip as it shows, `Segment.shownSize`; Mirror and Flip act on the picture as shown, `Segment.mirrored`; the canvas stays the first clip's size, a turned clip is fitted into it; the crop screen shows the frame mirrored and turned, `turnedCrop`/`unturnedCrop`; the stage's own players draw a clip through `ShapedClip`; the filmstrip stays unturned), Duplicate, Delete (not the last); Apply to all in the Speed, Filters and Adjust panels copies the clip's speed (video clips only) or its look and sliders to every clip (`updateAllSegments`); the size and frame rate come from the first clip and the others are fitted into its frame. Project-wide: songs (`VideoClip.music`, added at the playhead; Sound from a video adds a picked video as a song: its audio sequence item removes the video, so only its sound plays, and `addMusic` refuses a file without an audio track) and voiceovers recorded from the playhead in the Audio panel (`ui/editor/VoiceoverPanel.kt`; `VideoClip.voices`) or spoken there by text to speech (`ui/editor/SpeechDialog.kt`: a line and one of the phone's offline voices, `media/Speech.kt` `offlineVoices` (installed, `isNetworkConnectionRequired` false; none says how to install one) and `synthesizeSpeech` into a `.wav` in the project folder, `ProjectStore.newSpeechFile`), each an audio track under the clip strip with an `AudioPart` (`video/AudioParts.kt`: output start, offset into the source, length, volume, fade in and out lengths up to 5 s). Tap a track to select it (`EditorUiState.selectedAudio`, an `AudioRef`), drag its middle to move it, its ends to trim it (committed on release as one undo step: `AudioPart.dragged`), Split it at the playhead into two that sound the same (no fades at the cut: `splitAt`) or Delete it; the Audio panel then shows its volume, fades and, for a song, where in the song it starts, for a voiceover its voice effect (`VoiceClip.effect`, a `VoiceEffect` in `video/VoiceEffects.kt`: Normal, Higher, Deeper, Chipmunk (Sonic pitch, same length), Robot (ring modulation by a `ROBOT_HZ` sine as a `GainProcessor` gain); its processors go after the gain on the voice's audio sequence item, so the preview and the export match). A song loops from that point to the end of its part; a part of `Long.MAX_VALUE` runs to the end of the video, which is what an old project's single song reads as (`video/MusicFade.kt` for the repeats and fades). Volumes never reload the preview: its gains (`VolumeGain` over the fades) read the clips', songs' and voiceovers' volumes from a `LiveVolumes` (`composition(volumes =)`) on every sample, and a volume slider's drag goes straight into it; only muting every clip or unmuting one of them reloads, since that adds or drops the clips' audio track. Media3 mixes the sound about a second ahead, so a change while playing is heard that much later; paused, the editor seeks in place (`refreshPaused`, a millisecond off: a seek to the very spot leaves Media3 1.11 silent for a second) to drop it. Text/sticker layers sit on their own timeline tracks above the clip, each with a start and end time (a selected track shows trim handles at its ends; trimming a video layer's start moves its in-point with it, `VideoLayer.trimmedStart`), an eye and a lock at its left, and long-press-drag to reorder; a selected layer's Split cuts it at the playhead into two with the same look (no exit/entrance at the cut; a video layer's second part gets the later in-point) and Move here starts it at the playhead (`layerSplitPoint`, `layerMovedTo` in `video/Segments.kt`). Pinch the timeline to zoom, slide two fingers to scroll (view-only, `TimelineViewport`); one finger always edits or scrubs. Video layers (picture-in-picture) put more clips on top, placed like stickers, stacked with text and stickers in track order (a caption under a video layer's track is covered by it), with their own track, opacity, volume, in-point (`clipStartMs`, the Edit panel `ui/editor/VideoLayerPanel.kt`) and crop (`VideoLayer.crop`, a `CropRect` of the frame picked in the shared `CropScreen`; the layer's size is the cropped size, the export adds a Media3 `Crop` before its `Presentation`, the preview clips a full-frame `TextureView` to the layer box) and a green screen (the Chroma key tool, `ui/editor/ChromaKeyPanel.kt`: `VideoLayer.key`, a color or null for off, starting at `KEY_GREEN`, with `keyStrength` and `keySoftness`; `video/ChromaKey.kt` makes pixels near the key's chroma (Cb/Cr, so brightness doesn't count) transparent with a soft edge and takes the key's tint out of the ones close to it; the export puts it first in the layer's effects and the compositor shows what's under through its alpha, the preview runs the same effect on the layer's own `ExoPlayer` through `setVideoEffects`, premultiplied for its non-opaque `TextureView` (effects take only before the player prepares, so it gets a new player the first time it's keyed and keeps the effect after, a null key passing frames unchanged); the eyedropper picks from the selected video layer's raw frame, `stagePicture`); export H.264/AAC MP4 to `Movies/Kadar`, or a GIF to `Pictures/Kadar`.
- **Blur areas** (`CensorLayer` in `model/Layers.kt`, `video/Censor.kt`, `ui/editor/CensorPanel.kt`; the Blur area tool, videos only): a rectangle, ellipse or heart (`CensorShape`) that blurs or pixelates (`CensorKind`) the clips under it, as coarse as its `strength` (`censorCell`, a share of the canvas's short side), placed, resized (side handles stretch it), rotated and timed on its own track like any layer. It draws nothing (`drawContent` is empty, like a video layer's); its pixels are the `Censor` GL effect, the last of every clip's and incoming head's effects (after the fit, placement and bars, so its frame is the canvas and its time output time), which per frame takes the areas shown then (`CensorUniforms`, at most `MAX_CENSORS`): pixelate is each block's average, blur blends the averages of the four nearest cells smoothly. So it censors the main track only: video layers and every drawn layer (the overlay, after the clips) stay sharp, in the preview and the export alike. The preview's clips always get it through `LiveLooks.frameEffects` reading `LiveLooks.showCensors`, so moving or changing an area never reloads the preview (paused, the frame is redrawn); the heads of overlapping transitions get it the same way (`headsComposition`). Photos don't offer it.
- **Export dialog** (`ui/editor/ExportDialog.kt`, opened by Save and Share): a photo's format; a video's MP4 or GIF, and for MP4 the frame rate (24, 25, 30, 50, 60, starting at the clip's own `outputFrameRate`), the quality (starting at the Settings one) and the estimated size (`VideoClip.estimatedSize`: duration times Media3's bitrate for that quality, size and rate, plus AAC). The clip's own rate exports as before; any other copies the `VideoClip` with that `frameRate` and passes `composition(frameClock = true)`, so the frame clock (`blankStill()`) sets every output frame time even without video layers. GIF (`video/Gif.kt`): the same composition rendered by Transformer to a small MP4 (long side `GIF_LONG_SIDE` 480, `GIF_FPS` 10 through the frame clock), then `mp4ToGif` reads its frames with `MediaMetadataRetriever` and `GifWriter` writes them (per-frame 256-color median-cut palette, LZW); videos longer than `GIF_MAX_MS` (15 s) are refused.
- **Transitions** (`video/Transitions.kt`, GL effects in `video/TransitionEffects.kt`, `ui/editor/TransitionPanel.kt`): a button on every cut of the clip strip opens the panel: None, Dissolve, Black, White, Zoom, Blur, Spin, Flip, Glitch, Color split, Wipe/Slide/Push left or up, a length (0.2 to 1.5 s, at most half the shorter neighbour: `maxTransitionMs`) and Apply to all. Stored as `Segment.transition`, the transition into that clip (the first clip's is unused; a split's second half gets none). Kinds that `overlaps` (dissolve, wipes, slides, pushes) play the incoming clip's head over the end of the clip before, its sound crossfaded with that clip's (`crossfadeGain`, equal power), so the video gets that much shorter: `VideoClip.played` is the clips without their heads (`headsMs`), `outputDurationMs`, `segmentAt` and `segmentStart` count with it, and the clip strip draws the played clips. The others are centered on the cut, one clip at a time, and dip the sound to the cut and back (`dipEffects` on the clips either side: a spin turns the frame in its own proportions and zooms, `spinDegrees`/`spinScale`; a flip squeezes its width, `flipWidth`; glitch and color split are one shader, `ChannelShift`, red and blue moved apart and, for a glitch, bands of rows slid by a pattern from `glitchSeed` of the frame time, so the preview and the export match). `transitionSpans` says where each plays in output time.
- **Animations** (`model/Animation.kt`, `ui/editor/AnimationPanel.kt`): video text, sticker and shape layers get an entrance and an exit (None, Fade, Pop, Zoom out, Slide up/down/left/right; a slide moves `Motion.dx`/`dy`), 300 ms each unless set (`Layer.animInMs`/`animOutMs`, null for the kind's own length), at most half the layer's time, and a loop (`Loop`: Wiggle, Pulse, Bounce) all the time they show. Text also gets per-letter entrances (`Anim.perLetter`: Typewriter, Letters pop, 1.2 s): the `Motion` carries a `Reveal`, `letterScale` says how big each letter draws, and `TextLayer.drawContent(canvas, reveal)` draws the letters one by one at the layout's positions, so it stays the one text drawing path. `motionAt` turns a layer and an output time into alpha, scale, offset, rotation and reveal, and `Canvas.drawLayer(layer, motion, canvasHeight)` applies it around `drawLayer`; the stage uses it with the player position (every frame while playing), the export's `timedOverlay` with each frame's timestamp (a looping layer's stretch is drawn per frame all through).
- **Clip animations** (`video/ClipAnimation.kt`, GL effects `clipAnimEffects` in `video/TransitionEffects.kt`, `ClipAnimationPanel` in `ui/editor/AnimationPanel.kt`, the Animate tool of a picked clip): each main-track clip gets an entrance and an exit (`Segment.animIn`/`animOut`, a `ClipAnimation`: None, Fade, Zoom in, Zoom out, Slide left/right/up/down, Spin, 0.5 s unless set, at most half the clip as it plays: `maxClipAnimMs`). An exit is its entrance played backwards, except that slides and spins keep going their way. A transition at that cut plays instead (the panel says so): `clipAnimSpans` leaves those edges out, so heads of overlapping transitions never animate. On the canvas after the fit and before the placement (so a placed clip turns about its own center and a slide starts off the canvas at any zoom); a fade scales the clip's alpha (`BarFill`'s `alpha`), so the bars' color shows through, black too (`VideoClip.barFill` then gives black), or the blurred canvas, which itself doesn't animate. Item effects at output time like `dipEffects`, so the preview plays them as they export; a change reloads it like any structure change.
- **Text look** (`TextLayer`): `curve` (-1 to 1, the Style panel's Curve: each line along an arc of one circle around a shared center, `drawCurved` with `drawTextOnPath` through the letters' middle, so it stays the one text drawing path; `curveRadius`/`curveRise` in `model/Shapes.kt`; the block gets taller by the rise), a `gradient` color the letters fade into from `color` top to bottom (a `LinearGradient` on the fill pass only; `ui/editor/TextColorPanel.kt`), outline color and `outlineWidth` (`ui/editor/OutlinePanel.kt`), shadow (`shadow` color, `shadowBlur`, `shadowDistance`, all in multiples of `size`, `ui/editor/ShadowPanel.kt`; old projects' `"shadow": true` reads as `DEFAULT_SHADOW`), glow (`glow` color, `glowSize`, `ui/editor/GlowPanel.kt`), `boxOpacity` (the box or bubble fill only), format toggles `bold`/`italic` (the platform's `Typeface.create`, faked for one-style fonts), `underline` and `caps` (draws `text.uppercase()`, the stored text stays as typed) in the Style panel, a Size slider in the Font panel (the placement scale, like a pinch; 100% is `defaultTextSize`), opacity (the whole layer through one offscreen layer), `lineSpacing` and `wrap` (where the text wraps, in layer pixels; null keeps the lines as typed). The side handles of a selected text drag its wrap with the other edge kept in place (`TextLayer.wrapDragged`). Presets (`model/TextPresets.kt`, `ui/editor/TextPresetsPanel.kt`, tiles drawn with `drawLayer`) set font, colors, box/bubble, glow, shadow and spacing in one undo step.
- **Imported fonts**: Font > Add font copies a picked `.ttf`/`.otf` into `filesDir/fonts` (`media/Media.kt` `importFont`: size-capped, kept only if `loadFont` can load it), listed after the bundled fonts in `EditorUiState.fonts` and used in every project, like stickers. Projects store the font name (the file name); a missing one falls back to the default font.
- **Undo / redo** for every edit (gestures and timeline drags count once per drag).
- **Snapping** to the canvas center and 0°/90°, caption boxes behind text, a Layers panel (photos only, top-bar button; video shows layers as timeline tracks), keep-pitch for speed changes.
- **Speech bubbles** (`ui/editor/BubblePanel.kt`, geometry in `model/Shapes.kt`): a text layer's box has a `Bubble` style (the caption `Box`, or `Speech`, `Thought`, `Shout`, `Pill` (a capsule, lines kept as typed), `Double` (two outline lines: one wide stroke with the fill color down its middle)) with a fill (`box`) and an outline (`boxOutline`). Bubbles wrap the text (`bubbleWrapWidth`) into an ellipse, and their tail points at `TextLayer.tail` (layer pixels from its center; null is the default spot), dragged with a third handle on the stage (`Transform.toLocal`). Body and tail are merged into one `Path` (`Path.op`), so the outline never crosses the inside. `Layer.reach` covers the tail, so a fade's offscreen layer doesn't clip it.
- **Shapes** (`ShapeLayer` in `model/Layers.kt`, points in `model/Shapes.kt`, `ui/editor/ShapePanel.kt`): rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse, arrow, star and line, `w`×`h` in their own pixels (the side handles change them, `ShapeLayer.sideDragged`, so the outline keeps its width), with a fill and an outline (either none), an outline width and an opacity (one offscreen layer, so the outline doesn't show through the fill). The Shape tool adds one (`newShape`); with a shape selected it changes the kind, the stretch (width to height) and the opacity.
- **Drawing** (`model/Strokes.kt`, `ui/editor/DrawScreen.kt`): the Draw tool opens a full-screen pad (one finger draws, two zoom/pan) with marker, highlighter, neon, dotted, spray and soft pens (`PenStroke.style`, a `PenStyle` drawn by `DrawLayer.drawContent`: neon a blurred glow plus a light core, dotted round-capped zero-length dashes, soft a blurred edge, spray seeded dots from `sprayDots`; `PenStroke.reach` covers the glow), an eraser (`erased`: takes out the points it reaches, so it splits strokes and keeps them vectors), colors, size, opacity and undo/redo (whole stroke lists, strokes are immutable). Apply makes one `DrawLayer` (strokes in its own pixels, centered on its origin by `withStrokes`, smoothed by `smoothSegments`); Draw on a selected drawing edits it: its strokes come in as canvas pixels (`canvasStrokes`) and go back with its rotation and scale kept; erased to nothing, the layer is deleted.
- **Filters** (`model/Looks.kt`, `ui/editor/FiltersPanel.kt`): in-house looks as 4x5 color matrices, blended by a strength and stored on `Adjust` (`look`, `amount`). `Adjust.filterMatrix()` (look, then the sliders) is the one matrix for the image layer paint and, through `glRgbMatrix`, the video's Media3 `RgbMatrix`, so photos and video match. The Adjust panel's sliders (brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, tint, hue, fade; one at a time, picked from tabs) are matrices too, and a color slider at 0 is left out, so old projects keep their exact matrix. Video layers have an `Adjust` of their own: the export puts its `RgbMatrix` first in the layer's effects, the preview sets the same matrix as the `TextureView`'s layer paint. Hold to compare (`CompareButton` in Filters and Adjust) shows `Project.withoutAdjust()` on the stage and the preview while pressed, view-only. A Filters or Adjust slider shows while it's dragged (`KSlider`'s `onDrag`; the release commits one undo step): the editor stages the value with `Project.withAdjust`, view-only, like compare. Looks never reload the video preview: its composition takes the clips' looks from a `LiveLooks` (`composition(looks =)`, `video/VideoEdit.kt`), read on every frame, so a look change swaps its matrices (and, paused, redraws the last frame from the player's replayable cache: `experimentalRedrawLastFrame`); the preview is rebuilt only when its structure changes (`VideoClip.previewStructure`: without the looks, the volumes, the clips' placement and bar color, or the blurred canvas, which the preview draws itself). The export bakes each clip's look in as before. Vignette and grain (`Adjust.vignette`/`grain`, 0..1, the last two Adjust tabs) aren't colors (`isNone` ignores them, `hasTexture` says if either is on): a photo's image layer draws them in `drawContent` as `texturePaints` (a radial gradient on the ellipse that fits the picture, a tiled noise bitmap of white and black specks), in SRC_ATOP on a `saveLayer`, so transparent pixels stay transparent; video gets the `VignetteGrain` GL effect after the color matrix (clips: before the fit into the canvas; video layers: after their crop and `Presentation`, so it fits what they show), with the same falloff (`vignetteDarkness`), speck size (`grainCell`, a share of the picture) and mix, new specks every frame. `LiveLooks.texture` gives every preview clip one, read per frame, so those sliders are live too; a video layer's preview draws `texturePaints` over its `TextureView` from a Compose `Canvas`, moved every played frame.
- **Eraser / restore brush and crop** for image layers (`ui/editor/ImageTools.kt`). The brush has a hardness (soft edge, a `BlurMaskFilter`) and undo/redo: strokes are kept and replayed onto the layer's bitmap, and each move repaints the stroke in progress over the picture from before it, so a soft edge doesn't build up. Crop has ratios, and for image layers quarter turns and straightening (±45°, an angle dial; `model/Crop.kt` for the math, `turnBitmap` in `media/`); a turned crop turns the source too (`turnedSourceOffset`), so restore still lines up, and bakes a flipped layer's flip into both (the new layer is unflipped). The image layers' crop also cuts to a shape (`CutShape`: circle, rounded, heart, star, triangle, diamond, filling the crop rectangle as shown; picking one from Free starts at 1:1): `cutToShape` in `media/` makes everything outside it transparent in the cropped bitmap, same size, so the source offsets and the restore brush hold and the picture outline follows the shape; nothing new is stored. `ImageLayer.source` + `sourceX/Y` remember the untouched original and where the layer's bitmap sits in it; keep them right in any new operation that resizes or crops a bitmap.
- **Settings** (`model/Settings.kt`, `ui/home/SettingsScreen.kt`): image search engine, default export size (per-project override in the Canvas panels), video export quality (Standard is Media3's bitrate, High is `highBitrate` in `video/VideoEdit.kt`), the home grid's sort order, About with the bundled license texts (`third_party/` is packaged as app assets).
- **Share to Kadar**: `MainActivity` (singleTask) takes `ACTION_SEND` images and videos and calls `EditorViewModel.openShared`.
- **Projects**: every edit autosaves into the app's private storage and shows up on the home screen; reopen, or long-press to select and duplicate, rename or delete. Each has an optional `name` in `project.json` (unnamed ones show "Photo · Oct 12" from the type and the folder's creation timestamp); `ProjectStore.rename` edits only that field and keeps the file's modified time. The grid sorts by Recent / Name / Type (videos first), remembered in `AppSettings.projectSort`, and gets a search field past 6 projects; the filtering and sorting is `model/ProjectList.kt`.
- **Web images**: an in-app browser (Google Images by default). Long-press an image to download it into the project, never into the gallery.
- **My stickers**: cut-outs saved for reuse across projects.

## Stack

- Kotlin, Jetpack Compose with Material 3, one `:app` module
- AGP 9.4 (built-in Kotlin), Kotlin 2.4, Gradle 9.6.1 wrapper
- `compileSdk 37`, `targetSdk 35`, `minSdk 29`
- Media3 Transformer for video export and `CompositionPlayer` for the live preview (the same `Composition` drives both)
- ML Kit Subject Segmentation (Play services, on-device) for background removal
- `org.json` for project files, Coroutines/Flow for async
- No DI framework, no navigation library: `MainActivity` builds one `EditorViewModel` with `viewModel()` and switches screens on `uiState.project` plus a `browsing` flag
- **Not Material-looking**: Kadar's own dark UI with a lime accent from `ui/common/Theme.kt` (`Ink` colors, Plus Jakarta Sans, `IconTool`, `CircleButton`, `KSlider`, `ColorRow`, `TextTabs`, `KDialog`, `KTextField`). Material 3 is only the plumbing underneath; don't use stock M3 buttons, chips or top bars in screens.
- Icons are Material Symbols Rounded (weight 300) vector drawables `res/drawable/ic_*.xml`, added with `scripts/add-icons.sh <name>...`

Versions live in `gradle/libs.versions.toml`.

### Where new code goes

| You're adding | Put it in |
|---|---|
| A field on a layer, project, clip or setting | The type in `model/` or `video/`, plus its JSON read/write in `model/Projects.kt` (`opt*` default) |
| A new layer type | `model/Layers.kt` (type + `drawContent`), `model/Projects.kt` (JSON), then UI |
| Math, geometry, matrices, time calculations | A plain function in the matching `model/` or `video/` file (or a new focused file there) + a test in `app/src/test` under the same package |
| Anything that touches files, MediaStore, the network or ML Kit | `media/` |
| Anything that builds or exports video | `video/` |
| A widget used by two screens | `ui/common/Theme.kt` (or a new file in `ui/common/` if it's big) |
| A new editor panel or overlay screen | Its own file in `ui/editor/` (`EditorScreen.kt` should shrink, not grow) |
| A new top-level screen | Its own file in the `ui/` package of its area, or a new `ui/<area>/` package |
| State and actions | `EditorViewModel` (orchestrates; delegates logic to `model/`, `media/`, `video/`) |
| Any text the user sees | Both `res/values/strings.xml` and `res/values-bs/strings.xml`; from outside the UI as a `UiText` / `UserError` |
| A new language | `res/values-<lang>/strings.xml` with every entry, plus a `<locale>` in `res/xml/locales_config.xml` |

Dependencies point one way: `ui/*` may use `model`, `media`, `video` and `EditorViewModel`; `model/` uses nothing from the app (string resource ids from `R` are fine); `media/` and `video/` use only `model/`. Never import `ui` from `model`, `media` or `video`.

## Rules that matter

1. **One drawing path.** The stage, the photo export, the video overlay and the thumbnails all go through `drawLayer`/`renderLayers`. Never draw a layer type a second way, or exports stop matching the screen. The one exception is `VideoLayer`: its pixels come from a video player (the export's Media3 compositor, the stage's `VideoLayerPreview`), so its `drawContent` draws nothing and the stage canvas draws just its selection box. `CensorLayer` is the same: its pixels are the `Censor` effect on the clips.
2. **Canvas pixels are the unit.** Layer transforms are in full-resolution canvas pixels (photo size or video display size). The stage only scales for display; gestures divide by that scale.
3. **Never lose a project.** Project writes go through `writeAtomic` (write then rename) under `saveLock`. A missing image drops only its layer, never the whole project. If you change the JSON format, bump `version` in `Project.toJson`, keep reading older files, and default new fields with `opt*`.
4. **Images live in the project folder.** Every bitmap a layer or a photo clip uses is written once with `ProjectStore.writeImage` and referenced by file name; `ProjectStore.cleanUp` removes unreferenced ones on close. Voice recordings are `.m4a` files in the same folder (`ProjectStore.newVoiceFile`; spoken text `.wav`, `newSpeechFile`), stored by name in `VideoClip.voices`; `cleanUp` keeps every one the project references. Videos and music are referenced by content URI with a persisted read grant (`keepAccess`); only when that fails (a one-time share grant) is the video copied into the project as `video.mp4`.
5. **Nothing goes to the gallery except an export.** Web downloads, cut-outs and stickers stay in app storage. Exports go to `Pictures/Kadar` or `Movies/Kadar` through `saveToGallery` (no storage permission needed).
6. **Offline first.** No analytics, no accounts, no cloud APIs. The browser is the only feature that needs internet; don't add features that silently require it.
7. **Treat web input as untrusted.** `downloadImage` accepts only `http(s)` and base64 `data:` URLs, caps the size, and must fail with a message, not a crash.
8. **The preview is the export.** Video settings change `VideoClip`; `VideoClip.composition()` builds both the `CompositionPlayer` preview and the Transformer export. Add new video features there, not in only one of them. Media3's `DefaultVideoCompositor` takes every output frame time from its primary input (sequence 0) and always draws it on top, so with video layers sequence 0 is the frame clock (`blankStill()`), a blank still at the clip's frame rate (`VideoClip.frameRate`) drawn at alpha 0; then the video layers (topmost first), padded with gaps to the output's length (the clips' output lengths added up), gap frames hidden by alpha, each followed by the drawn layers right under it in the list if any (`drawnGroups`): another blank still, presented at the canvas size, with those layers as a `timedOverlay` and `Unpremultiply` (the overlay on a transparent frame comes out premultiplied and the compositor blends straight alpha), full-frame in the compositor; then, with overlapping transitions, the incoming heads (`incomingSequence`: one sequence, a gap, a head, a gap and so on, each head playing `headTailsMs` past its cut so the frame before the cut never pairs with a hidden gap frame, faded or moved by `incomingSettings` and wiped by the `WipeMask` item effect; a push moves the clips' input by `outgoingSettings`; the compositor pairs each output frame with the nearest frame of every input and evaluates settings at that input frame's own time, so placements go through `outputFrameUs`; anchors only take -1..1, so a whole canvas away is both anchors at half, `shifted`); then the clips, one item per played `Segment`, each first cropped, mirrored and turned (`shapeEffects`; `BlurBackgroundPreview` does the same to its view with `ShapedClip`) (a photo is an image item with `setImageDurationMs` and the frame rate; clips of another size are fitted with a `Presentation`; with sound in any clip the sequence declares an audio track so photos get silence; the centered transitions are item effects on the clips either side of the cut, `dipEffects`, which Media3 hands frames at their time in the sequence, output time, in the preview and the export alike, and so are the clips' entrances and exits, `clipAnimEffects`, on the canvas after the fit); every clip (and incoming head) is then placed by `VideoClip.placement` (a `ClipPlacement`: zoom of at least the fit and an offset in canvas shares, a `MatrixTransformation` after the fit, which Media3 draws in the same pass) and gets its letterbox bars painted in the project's `background` color by `BarFill` (`VideoClip.barFill`: none for black or under the blurred canvas); the preview takes both from `LiveLooks.showFrame`, read per frame (its `BarFill` leaves see-through pixels premultiplied, as its `TextureView` blends them over the blurred canvas; Media3's compositor blends straight alpha), so a drag on the stage with the video Canvas panel open (one undo step per gesture) or a new color doesn't reload it, and so do the heads `IncomingClipPreview` plays; pictures drawn without Media3 (thumbnail, eyedropper, draw pad) put the frame at `VideoClip.frameRect`; with a blurred canvas (`VideoClip.blur`, its sigma `BLUR_SIGMA` times `VideoClip.blurStrength`, the preview's blur radius scaled the same) the next sequence, drawn under the clips, is the same clips again (muted, same trims and speeds), filled, blurred and darkened, showing through the clip's letterbox bars, which Media3 leaves transparent. Without video layers the clips are sequence 0. Audio-only sequences come last, one per repeat of each song part, then one per voiceover part (`audioSequences`): a gap until it plays, that stretch of the source, a gap to the end, so each repeat knows its output time for the fades; several items in one audio sequence left the single-input preview black. The layers above the topmost video layer (all of them without video layers, so those projects get no extra sequence) are the bitmap overlay on the composition, after compositing. The preview leaves video layers out of its composition: `ui/editor/VideoLayerPreview.kt` draws each with its own `ExoPlayer` in a `TextureView` over the main preview, in list order with a canvas of the drawn layers under it before each (the same `drawnGroups`) and under the top stage canvas (the layers above every video layer, selection, handles and gestures), placed from the same `Transform` and timed with the same `overlaySpan`/`overlaySourceMs` as the export, kept in step with the main player (`overlayNeedsSeek`), so a drag only moves a view. The blurred canvas is left out the same way (`ui/editor/BlurBackgroundPreview.kt`: the clips as a playlist in a muted `ExoPlayer` that seeks to the clip under the playhead and takes its speed, blurred and darkened with a `RenderEffect` on Android 12+ at 1/8 of its size and scaled up, behind the main preview, whose letterbox bars are transparent). The main preview is a `SurfaceView` (frames shown at their time without the UI thread; a `TextureView` judders and drops frames when the UI is busy), except with the blurred canvas, which needs a `TextureView` so the bars show what's under them. The stage redraws animated layers once per video frame (`frameStartMs`), not per screen refresh. Sped-up clips get a frame drop effect in the export when the clips are sequence 0, so 10x doesn't hand the encoder 300 fps; the preview leaves it out (`dropFrames = false`), since Media3 1.11's preview runs on past the end with it. The heads of overlapping transitions are left out too (`composition(incoming = false)`): `ui/editor/IncomingClipPreview.kt` plays them in a `CompositionPlayer` of its own (`VideoClip.headsComposition`: the export's `incomingSequence` alone, single-input, so the heads get the export's crop, look, fit, placement, bars, blur areas and wipe, with the live looks, volumes and preview copies of the main preview) in a `TextureView` over the main preview (under the video layers), faded and moved by the view through the same `TransitionKind` functions, seeks it while playing only past `HEAD_MAX_DRIFT_MS` (its seeks stall it far longer than an `ExoPlayer`'s), starts each a measured startup time early so it is in step when the transition starts, and keeps each on top at its final look for `PREVIEW_TAIL_MS` past its cut, since the main preview shows the clip before for a frame or two after switching clips, and pushes move the main preview's view. So every preview player stays single-input: Media3 1.11's multi-input preview fails mid-playback on devices. Media3 1.11's preview also stalls for good when it plays into an AV1 or VP9 clip that starts between keyframes (a trim, or a head an overlapping transition cuts off; the export is fine). So such clips get a preview copy: `EditorViewModel.loadPreviewCopy` checks a clip's codecs (`trackMimes`, `needsPreviewCopy`) and has Transformer make a 720p H.264 copy (`makePreviewCopy`, one at a time) in the project folder (`ProjectStore.previewCopyFile`, removed by `cleanUp` with its clip); the preview's `composition(previewCopies =)` plays it, fitted into the frame, and the export reads the source. Until a copy is ready, the editor seeks to the playhead when the player has been buffering for `STALL_MS` while playing. The editor keeps the screen on while the preview plays.
9. **Layer times are output time** (the clips end to end, each after its trim and speed, less the overlapping transitions; reordering or splitting clips or adding a transition leaves layers where they are in time), the same clock as `CompositionPlayer.currentPosition`. `endMs = Long.MAX_VALUE` means "to the end". The stage shows only layers visible at the playhead (plus the selected one); the export uses `timedOverlay`, which sits on the composition (not the clip) so it sees output timestamps (layers under a video layer: on a blank still from 0 with no speed change, the same clock). `timedOverlay` caches one bitmap per stretch where nothing changes and draws only animated stretches per frame (one reused bitmap, from layer copies, since that runs on the GL thread); never cache per-frame bitmaps.
10. **Every edit is undoable.** Change the project only through `updateProject`/`updateLayer`; continuous gestures pass `record = first` so one drag is one undo step.
11. **Long tasks go through `EditorViewModel.work`.** It shows the busy overlay (which keeps the screen on), runs one task at a time, and turns exceptions into a snackbar. A task can fill the overlay's ring by setting `progress` (percent) and offer Cancel by setting `cancellable` only while stopping is safe (video export: during the Transformer run, not the gallery copy); `cancelWork` cancels the coroutine, so cleanup goes in `finally`, and `work` always clears `busy`. Transformer must be called on the main thread.
12. **Match existing patterns.** State is one `StateFlow<EditorUiState>`, one-shot messages go through the `effects` channel, panels are composables in `ui/editor/` that call the ViewModel directly. Reuse the components in `ui/common/Theme.kt` before writing new widgets.
13. **Edge drags need `systemGestureExclusion()`**, or the system back gesture steals them (the timeline does this).
14. **Clean room, open assets.** Never copy, decompile or look at another editing app's code, and never take its icons, images, fonts, stickers, presets or distinctive UI design; build features from public descriptions of what they do. Every bundled asset must be openly licensed (OFL, CC0, Apache/MIT, own work) and get a row in `ATTRIBUTION.md` plus its license file in `third_party/`. Don't name other apps in the UI or store text.
15. **Known patents to stay clear of** (checked 2026-10-12, assignee search, not a legal opinion): US11989808B2 covers recording a creator's edit steps as a "replay" others apply to their own photo; read its claims before building anything like edit-sharing templates. USD1056949S1 is a design patent on a staggered two-column card feed with a five-tab bottom bar and a central round "+"; don't build the home screen that way.
16. **Smallest change that fully works.** No new dependencies, layers or abstractions without a clear need.
17. **No inline UI text.** Every user-visible string (labels, content descriptions, dialogs, snackbars) is a resource in `res/values/strings.xml` (English) and `res/values-bs/strings.xml` (Bosnian, Latin script); `lintDebug` fails on a missing translation. Composables use `stringResource` / `pluralStringResource` with format args, never concatenation. Text made outside the UI is a `UiText` (`model/UiText.kt`): busy labels and `EditorEffect.Message` carry one, and a failure the user should read is `throw UserError(R.string.x)`; other exception messages show as they are. Enum labels are `@StringRes` ids; names stored in projects or settings (enum names, `Font.name`) are data and never translated. Toolbar labels must fit a 68dp `IconTool` (about 11 characters at 11sp).

## Keep the codebase clean

Several agents work on this repo in parallel, so structure matters as much as features.

1. **Reuse before you write.** Search for an existing helper first: shared UI pieces live in `ui/common/Theme.kt` (`IconTool`, `CircleButton`, `KSlider`, `ColorRow`, `TextTabs`, `KToggle`, `KDialog`, `KTextField`, `PanelLabel`), drawing in `model/Layers.kt`, media I/O in `media/Media.kt`, video in `video/VideoEdit.kt`, persistence in `model/Projects.kt`. Extend one of those instead of writing a second copy. If you need something twice, extract it once to the right file.
2. **One responsibility per file, in the right package.** Follow "Where new code goes" above. A new screen or self-contained component gets its own file. `ui/editor/EditorScreen.kt` is the biggest file: put new editor parts (panels, timeline pieces, overlays) in their own files instead of growing it; move code out only when you are already changing it, as its own commit.
3. **Pure logic outside composables and the ViewModel.** Math, geometry, parsing, matrix and time calculations go in plain Kotlin functions in `model/` or `video/` with a JVM test in `app/src/test` under the same package. Composables render and forward events; the ViewModel orchestrates and owns state.
4. **Model types live with their data.** Layer types and their drawing in `model/Layers.kt`; `Project` and its JSON in `model/Projects.kt`; video settings in `video/VideoEdit.kt`; app settings in `model/Settings.kt`. A new field touches its type, its JSON read/write (with an `opt*` default) and nothing else by surprise.
5. **Name things after what they are**, match the existing naming and formatting, no dead code, no commented-out code, no `TODO` without context. Comments explain *why*, never *what*.
6. **Small, focused diffs.** Don't reformat or reorganize code you aren't changing; it makes parallel work impossible to merge. Refactors and file moves are their own commits, and a move commit changes nothing but `package`/`import` lines.
7. **Keep this document true.** When you add a file, a package or a pattern, update "Layout" and "Where new code goes" in the same commit.

## Verifying a change

1. `./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug` passes.
2. Install on the emulator and try the affected flow end to end, including **Export** and checking the file (`adb pull` it).
3. For project-format changes: open an existing project from before the change and confirm nothing is lost.
4. For video changes: export, then check the result with `ffprobe` (duration, rotation) and look at a frame.
