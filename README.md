*Kadar* (KAH-dar) is Bosnian for a frame or a shot in film. The app started because a meme page needed
the everyday editing tools, removing a background, slapping a caption on, speeding up a clip, adding
music, without paying a monthly fee for them. It grew into a general editor that runs entirely on the
phone.

## Features

### Photo

- **Layers.** Photos, images, text, emoji, shapes and stickers stack as layers. Drag to move, pinch to
  resize, twist to rotate, use the corner handle to scale and rotate with one finger, or stretch one way
  with the handle on each side. Mirror, flip and fade any picture.
- **Background remover.** One tap cuts the subject out of any image, on the device. The cut-out stays
  exactly where it was on the canvas, and can get a solid, glow or shadow outline around its shape.
- **Eraser and restore brush** with a soft or hard edge. Restore paints the original image back in
  place. Two fingers zoom and pan while you work.
- **Crop** any image freely or at a fixed ratio, turn and straighten it, or cut it to a circle, heart,
  star, triangle or diamond. Cropping the photo you started from crops the whole canvas.
- **Filters, adjust and effects.** Eight looks with a strength slider; brightness, contrast, saturation,
  warmth, tint, hue, fade, vignette and grain; blur, pixelate, posterize, duotone, sharpen, glitch and
  pop art; blend modes (multiply, screen, overlay and more) for pictures, text, shapes and drawings
  alike. Hold to compare with the original.
- **Draw.** Marker, highlighter, neon, dotted, spray and soft pens. Each drawing is a layer you can move,
  resize and draw more onto.
- **Shapes.** Rectangles, rounded rectangles, ellipses, arrows, stars and lines, with fill and outline.
- **Text.** A classic meme font, eight more free caption fonts, the system fonts and your own (.ttf or
  .otf). Fill, gradient, outline, shadow and glow; bold, italic, underline and capitals; curved text and
  a perspective slant; a caption box or a speech, thought, shout, pill or double-outline bubble with a
  tail you can point; one-tap style presets. Double-tap text to edit it.
- **Snapping.** Layers snap to the center of the canvas and to 0° and 90°, with guide lines.
- **Canvas.** Original size or 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 and more, on a color, a blurred copy of the photo or a
  transparent background (exported as PNG), with an optional frame around the edge and a drop shadow under
  the picture. Start from a photo or a blank canvas.
- **My stickers.** Save any cut-out and reuse it in every project, and start from the bundled pack of
  flat stickers.

### Video

- **Clips in a row.** Add videos and photos, trim them by their ends (step frame by frame), split,
  duplicate, reorder, replace, mute, freeze a frame, save a frame as a photo, add a plain color clip, and
  crop, rotate, mirror or flip a clip. Each clip has its own speed, volume, look and entrance and exit
  animation.
- **Speed** from 0.2x to 10x, with the voice pitch kept natural (or not, for the chipmunk effect).
- **Transitions** between clips: dissolve, dip to black or white, zoom, blur, spin, flip, glitch, color
  split, and wipes, slides and pushes, with the sound crossfaded.
- **A timeline with layer tracks.** Every caption, sticker, drawing and clip on top gets its own track
  above the main clip. Drag its ends to choose when it appears and disappears, drag the middle to move it,
  split it, long-press to reorder, and hide or lock it from the left. Pinch to zoom in on long videos.
- **Video over video.** Put more clips on top, picture-in-picture style: move, resize, rotate, crop and
  fade them, start them from the middle, give them their own volume and look, or key out a green screen.
- **Blur areas.** Blur, pixelate, frost, magnify, triangle-mosaic or halftone faces and usernames with a
  rectangle, ellipse or heart on its own track, each as strong or subtle as you like.
- **Animations.** Captions, stickers and shapes fade, pop, zoom or slide in and out, type out letter by
  letter, or wiggle, pulse and bounce.
- **Music and sound.** Add songs or the sound of another video, move and trim them on their own tracks,
  set their volume (up to 200%) and fades; they loop to fill their part. Sound effects come bundled — tap
  one and it lands at the playhead. Record a voiceover from the playhead, or have the phone's offline
  voice read a line, with higher, deeper, chipmunk or robot voice effects.
- **Canvas.** Turn a landscape clip into 9:16 for reels and stories, 1:1, 4:5 or 1:2, zoom and move the
  clip inside it, with colored bars, a blurred copy of the video, any picture or a gradient behind it.
- **Filters and adjust** per clip, the same looks as for photos, shown live while you drag.
- **What you see is what you export.** The preview runs the same edit the exporter does.

### Everywhere

- **Projects save automatically** and reopen exactly as you left them.
- **Undo and redo** for every edit.
- **Images from the web.** A built-in browser: search, long-press an image, and it goes straight into
  the project. It never lands in your gallery.
- **Share to Kadar** from the gallery or any app to start a project.
- **Export** to `Pictures/Kadar` and `Movies/Kadar` (JPEG, PNG, H.264 MP4 or GIF), or straight to the
  share sheet. Pick the frame rate and quality of a video and see its size before saving; exports show
  their progress and can be cancelled.
- **English and Bosnian**, following the phone's language.

## Privacy

Kadar has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and it never uploads your photos or videos.

- Editing and export run entirely on the phone.
- The **only** feature that uses the internet is the image browser, and only while you use it.
- Background removal uses Google ML Kit on the device. The first time, Google Play services downloads
  the model (a few MB); after that it works offline. Your images are not sent anywhere.
- The microphone is used only while you record a voiceover, and Android asks first. Recordings stay
  inside the project. Text to speech uses only the voices installed on the phone that work offline.
- Projects live in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them; your exports in the
  gallery stay.

## Install

1. Download `kadar-vX.Y.Z.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/n-delic/kadar/releases/latest).
   Each release also has a `.sha256` file if you want to check the download.
2. Open the APK on your phone. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file
   manager; allow it for that app only.
3. Updates install over the old version and keep your projects, because every release is signed with
   the same key.

**Requirements:** Android 10 or newer. Background removal needs Google Play services (standard on
almost every phone sold outside China).

## Build from source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/n-delic/kadar.git
cd kadar
echo "sdk.dir=$HOME/Android/Sdk" > local.properties
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug     # tests + debug APK
./gradlew installDebug                        # install "Kadar Dev" on a connected phone or emulator
```

You need JDK 17 or newer and the Android SDK with platform 37. Debug builds install as a separate app,
**Kadar Dev**, so they never touch your real projects. Release signing and the full setup are in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## How it works

- **Kotlin and Jetpack Compose**, one module, no DI framework. The UI is custom-drawn on top of Compose.
- **One drawing path.** The editor, photo export, video overlay and thumbnails all draw layers with the
  same Android `Canvas` code, so exports match the screen.
- **Media3.** The video edit is a Media3 `Composition`: trim, speed, pitch, volume, music and canvas
  size are effects on it. `CompositionPlayer` previews it and `Transformer` exports it, with timed
  layers rendered as an overlay.
- **ML Kit Subject Segmentation** for cut-outs.
- **Projects** are a folder per project with a `project.json` (versioned, written atomically) and the
  images they use.

The code map and the rules for changing it are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md); plans are in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## FAQ

**Is it really free?** Yes. No paid tier, no watermark, no ads. It's open source under the GPL.

**Why isn't it on Google Play?** Not yet. For now releases are published here as APKs.

**Can I use images from the web browser in my posts?** That depends on the image. Kadar only makes
downloading easier; you are responsible for having the right to use what you import.

**Does it work offline?** Everything except the image browser, and the one-time background-remover download.
